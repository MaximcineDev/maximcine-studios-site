The Studio

Maximcine Studios

Maximcine Studios is an independent games studio building small, focused mobile games — the kind you open for five minutes and stay for twenty.

Nebula Strike: Galactic Assault is our first release, built with Capacitor for Android with a strong emphasis on tight controls, fast feedback, and a game that respects your time.

We're currently prototyping our next project — more on that soon.
