The Studio

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Nebula Strike free to play?

Yes — Nebula Strike: Galactic Assault is free to download and play on Android, with optional in-app purchases.

What Android version do I need?

Nebula Strike runs on modern Android devices available through Google Play. Check the Play Store listing for the exact minimum OS version.

How long is a typical run?

Runs are designed to be short — most sessions last just a few minutes, so it's easy to play in quick bursts throughout the day.

Does Nebula Strike have daily rewards?

Yes — log in each day to claim a bonus and keep your ship upgraded without heavy grinding.
